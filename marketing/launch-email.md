# Launch email to the waitlist

Send this once NOC is on the App Store. Put the real App Store link where it says
`[APP STORE LINK]`. The waitlist only stores email addresses, not which language
someone signed up in, so send one email with the Dutch text first and the English
below it, separated by a line ("English below"). Afterwards, delete the waitlist
addresses (`npm run waitlist:export`, then `npm run waitlist:delete -- <csv>`), as the
privacy policy promises.

Send it as a plain email from the NOC address, in BCC (never visible to each
other), or through a free mailing tool that keeps addresses private.

---

## English

**Subject:** NOC is out. Go do something you haven't done yet.

**Preview text:** The NEVERMADE OUTDOOR CLUB app is on the App Store.

Hi,

You asked us to let you know when the app was ready. It is.

NEVERMADE OUTDOOR CLUB is now on the App Store for iPhone:
[APP STORE LINK]

What you can do with it:

- **Record your outings** with GPS: walks, runs, hikes and rides.
- **Find routes wherever you are.** NOC shows the waymarked walking, running and cycling routes around you, on its own map.
- **Collect your firsts.** First summit, first trail run, first night outside: NOC keeps track of the things you've never done before.
- **Do it together.** Follow friends, share what you did, and join the club on Discord.

It's free to download. Android comes next.

We built this for people who'd rather go somewhere new than go faster. Tell us what you think: reply to this email, or find us on Discord.

See you outside,
NEVERMADE OUTDOOR CLUB

*You're getting this one email because you joined the waitlist on nevermadeoutdoorclub.com. We're deleting the waitlist now, so you won't hear from us this way again.*
